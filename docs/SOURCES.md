# Sources and editorial basis

Prepared 23 October 2026. Links are references, not a claim of endorsement or permission to reproduce their images.

## The work and its neighbouring projects

- Kasper Bergholt, [Sand Drift Monument](https://bergholt.net/sand). Artist's account, inscription texts, translations, personal memory and historical context.
- [Flora Excursoria Hafniensis](https://bergholt.net/flora). Context for historical language, botanical naming and languages sharing a printed surface.
- [Flora repository](https://github.com/bergholt/flora-excursoria-hafniensis). Reference for the artist's practice of publishing transcriptions, executable analysis and explicit limitations together.
- [Sun: Triptychs for Solar Eclipses](https://bergholt.net/sun) and [Triptych repository](https://github.com/bergholt/triptych). Context for system poetry, fixed constraints and letter-based harmonics. Connections to Sand in the new description are editorial interpretations.
- [GitHub profile](https://github.com/bergholt). The profile was not directly retrievable during preparation; the Flora and Triptych repositories were inspected independently.

## NetLock records

- [NetLock account](https://bergholt.net/netlock). Artist's recollections and archival chronology. Weekly nodelist dates are dates of surviving records, not necessarily dates of the underlying events.
- [Pouët: NetLock BBS](https://www.pouet.net/boards.php?which=4495). Lists Kasper Bergholt as sysop and MS-DOS as platform; not evidence of a Sand demo release.
- [Demozoo: NetLock](https://demozoo.org/bbs/14053/). Records a Hillerød-area board, staff alias Fenger, and a reference to an October 1994 GSN infopacket. Its staff entry differs in form from the full name in other records. No unverified alias equivalence is asserted here.
- Anders Wegge Jakobsen, [Liste over danske BBS'er](https://da.wikisource.org/wiki/Liste_over_danske_BBS%27er). The NetLock row lists Kasper Bergholt Kristiansen, PC-only operation, 14,400 bps / V32B, round-the-clock access and FidoNet address 2:236/444. Historical telephone numbers are unnecessary for the project description and have not been copied into it.

The attached Google AI Overview screenshot is not treated as an independent source. In particular, public Maximus source under GPL is not evidence that the original NetLock-specific menus or the Sand code share that licence.
